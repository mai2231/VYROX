1. Demonstration terms

This VYROX build is a Phase 1 front-end demonstration. Products, prices, orders, customers, stock, reviews and reports are sample data. The admin console is intentionally unauthenticated and must not be exposed as a production system.

Placing a “demo order” creates a local record only. It is not an offer to sell, does not reserve stock and does not create a binding contract. No money is captured and no goods will be shipped.

2. Orders and pricing

Prices are displayed in Philippine pesos and include VAT. Prices, stock and availability may change. In a live store, an order is accepted only when a written confirmation is issued after payment verification and stock allocation.

We may refuse or cancel an order for obvious pricing errors, suspected fraud, stock unavailability or breach of these terms. Where a payment was captured, a refund would be issued to the original method.

3. Payments

The interface supports GCash, Maya, bank transfer and cash on delivery. In Phase 1 these are visual and validation-only flows. Payment status remains “Awaiting verification” or “Unpaid · on delivery” until a demo admin manually verifies it.

Never send money to the demo account details shown on this site. Live payment credentials and card numbers must never be entered into this demonstration.

4. Shipping and delivery

  • Metro Manila standard shipping is ₱99; provincial standard is ₱149.
  • Orders of ₱2,500 or more ship standard free within the Philippines.
  • Express delivery adds ₱150 and targets 1–2 days where serviceable.
  • Estimated windows are not guarantees. Delays caused by couriers, weather or incorrect addresses are outside our control.

Risk passes on delivery to the address provided. Please check the parcel on receipt and report visible damage promptly.

5. Returns and exchanges

Unused items in original packaging may be returned within 30 days of delivery. Beauty products must be unopened for hygiene reasons. Worn, washed, altered or damaged-by-use items are not eligible. Refunds are issued to the original payment method after inspection; shipping fees are refundable only where the return results from our error.

6. Accounts

Phase 1 does not create accounts or store credentials. A live account would require accurate information, safeguarding of your password and prompt notice of unauthorised use. We would never ask for your password by email or message.

7. Acceptable use
